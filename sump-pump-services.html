Keep the basement dry when the snow melts.

Your sump pump might sit idle for months, then run nonstop for a week in April. Checking it before it's needed is the best way to avoid finding out it failed.

01Common problems

What sump pump services usually looks like.

01

Pump won't start

No response when water rises in the pit.

02

Constant cycling

Short, frequent on-off cycles.

03

Strange noises

Grinding, rattling or humming without pumping.

04

Water in the basement

Water near the pit or along walls during storms or snowmelt.

East Central Minnesota noteSpring snowmelt and rising groundwater make March and April the busiest months for sump pumps across the region.

02Relevant causes

Why it happens.

  • Stuck or failed float switch
  • Worn motor or impeller
  • Clogged inlet or discharge line
  • Frozen or blocked discharge outlet in winter

03General approach

How service works.

  1. Inspect the system

    Check the pit, float, check valve and discharge route.

  2. Test operation

    Confirm the pump starts, empties the pit and shuts off properly.

  3. Repair or replace

    Replace failed components or size a new pump for the pit and inflow.

  4. Discuss backup

    Talk through backup options for power outages.

04Equipment considerations

What we look at.

Check valve

Prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit.

Discharge line

Should carry water well away from the foundation and be protected from freezing.

Backup options

Battery or water-powered backups can help during outages.

FAQSump Pump Services

Common questions.

General information — every situation is different.

When might a sump pump need attention?

Before spring snowmelt, after long idle periods, or if it cycles constantly, makes unusual noise or fails to start.

How can I test my sump pump?

Slowly pour water into the pit until the float rises; the pump should start and empty the pit. If it doesn't, request service.

Why does my pump run constantly?

High groundwater, a failed check valve or a float problem can all cause frequent cycling.
